Duel of the Four Sigils

EMBERFALL

A one-on-one card duel against Captain Vex. Spend energy to summon creatures of Ember, Tide, Gale and Stone, chain same-sigil Surge combos, hide behind Guards, and burn the enemy hero from 20 life to zero — before round 12 ends and the higher life total wins.

Goal

Reduce Captain Vex from 20 life to 0. If round 12 ends first, the higher life total wins.

Your turn

You gain +1 max energy each round (up to 8) and draw a card. Click a card in your hand, then a glowing slot to summon it. Creatures attack from the next turn unless they have Swift. Click a glowing creature, then an enemy creature or Vex himself. Guard creatures must be attacked first; Shield ignores one hit; Drain heals your hero; Surge grows stronger beside allies of the same sigil — build around one sigil or splash them all.

Controls

Mouse: click cards, slots and targets. Keyboard: 1–7 pick a hand card, Q/W/E choose a slot, Space ends the turn, Esc cancels, H help, Enter starts, R rematch.
